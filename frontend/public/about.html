About NRORA

NRORA Road Assistance is a digital-first roadside assistance platform designed to make membership, customer records, secure payments and assistance coordination simple and organised.

Who We Are

NRORA brings roadside assistance operations and digital customer services together in one connected system. Customers can register their vehicle, choose a membership, complete secure payment and receive digital confirmation.

The platform is designed to support customers and the NRORA operations team with clear records, service requests and coordinated assistance.

Our Purpose

To make roadside assistance easier to access and easier to manage through a dependable digital experience.

From membership registration to assistance coordination, every step should be clear and straightforward.
