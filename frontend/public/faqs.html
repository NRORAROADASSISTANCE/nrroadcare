Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about NRORA membership and roadside assistance.

Which vehicles are eligible for NRORA?
NRORA membership is for eligible private cars and four-wheelers only. Bikes, scooters, two-wheelers and commercial vehicles are not covered.
What are the membership prices?
Standard membership is ₹4,999 per year for up to 3 eligible roadside assistance requests. Pro membership is ₹6,999 per year for up to 5 eligible requests. Membership validity is 12 months from activation.
When can I start using the service?
Service eligibility begins after payment is successfully verified and your membership is activated. Please wait for activation confirmation before requesting service.
What roadside services can I request?
Depending on the situation and service availability, eligible requests may include battery jump-start, flat-tyre assistance, basic on-spot mechanical help, key-lockout assistance and towing/recovery coordination.
Are spare parts and towing charges included?
No. Spare parts, garage repair bills, towing/vehicle transport and third-party charges are generally payable by the customer. Confirm any applicable charges before authorizing extra work.
Can I get a refund or reimbursement if I arrange my own mechanic?
Please contact NRORA before arranging outside help. Costs incurred without NRORA's approval are not automatically reimbursed. Membership refund requests are handled under the Terms & Conditions and applicable law.
Do unused service requests carry forward?
No. Unused requests expire when the 12-month membership period ends and do not carry forward to the next year.
Is NRORA an insurance or accident-compensation plan?
No. NRORA provides roadside assistance; it is not motor insurance and does not provide accident compensation.
What should I do if I need assistance?
Use the NRORA customer/service channel and provide your registered mobile number, vehicle details and current location so the team can check your membership and coordinate eligible assistance.
