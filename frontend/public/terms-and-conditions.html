Membership Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms before purchasing an NRORA Road Assistance membership.

1. Membership plans, price and validity

Standard Membership costs ₹4,999 per year and includes up to three (3) eligible roadside assistance service requests. Pro Membership costs ₹6,999 per year and includes up to five (5) eligible roadside assistance service requests. The displayed plan price is the total amount payable by the customer. Membership is valid for 12 months from activation.

2. Eligible vehicles

Membership is available only for eligible private cars and private four-wheelers accepted by NRORA. Bikes, scooters, other two-wheelers, commercial vehicles, trucks, buses and other ineligible vehicle categories are not covered.

3. Activation — no waiting period

There is no separate membership waiting period. Service eligibility begins immediately after the payment is successfully verified and the membership is activated. A payment screenshot or an initiated transaction alone does not confirm activation. Membership validity is 12 months from the activation date.

4. Contact NRORA first

Whenever an eligible roadside problem occurs, the customer must contact NRORA through its official support channel first. NRORA will check membership eligibility and coordinate a mechanic. Costs for services arranged independently without NRORA's prior approval are not automatically reimbursable.

5. Included roadside assistance and service count

NRORA coordinates a mechanic to inspect the eligible vehicle and attempt a suitable roadside repair where safe and practical. Standard includes up to three (3) and Pro up to five (5) eligible service requests during the membership year. The allowance can be used for different eligible problems; it is not limited to one type of fault. Each separate eligible call/dispatch requiring a service visit counts as one request. A roadside repair or permanent repair cannot be guaranteed in every case.

6. Spare parts and repair materials

Spare parts, batteries, tyres, consumables, replacement components, repair materials and other parts or purchases required to fix the vehicle are charged separately to the customer unless expressly stated otherwise in writing. The customer will be informed of known additional charges and asked for approval before chargeable work or purchases are arranged, where reasonably possible.

7. Major repairs, towing and garages

If the mechanic determines that the problem cannot safely or practically be resolved at the roadside, NRORA will arrange or coordinate a suitable towing vehicle to a nearby garage with the customer's permission. Towing/recovery charges, garage inspection and labour charges, storage fees, parts and all other garage or third-party costs are payable by the customer and are not included in the membership price. The customer should be told the known charges and approve the destination and charges before arrangements are made, where reasonably possible.

8. No insurance or payment of major repair bills

NRORA is a roadside assistance membership, not an insurance policy. It does not cover accident compensation, vehicle damage, medical costs, insurance claims, major garage repairs, replacement parts, towing charges or reimbursement of bills unless a particular item is expressly confirmed as included in writing.

9. Availability and response

Service availability depends on location, coverage, mechanic availability, road access, weather, safety and local conditions. NRORA will make reasonable efforts to coordinate assistance, but cannot guarantee a specific arrival time or that every fault can be repaired roadside. In an emergency involving immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service.

10. Unused services and expiry

Any unused service requests expire at the end of the 12-month membership period and cannot be carried forward, transferred or exchanged for cash. Renewal is required after expiry to receive services for another membership year.

11. Cancellation and refund policy

Once the membership is activated, the membership fee is generally non-refundable, including where the customer does not use the included services. If the same payment is debited twice, the duplicate amount will be refunded after verification. Payment or activation errors will be investigated and corrected as appropriate. Nothing in this policy excludes any refund, remedy or consumer right that must be provided under applicable law.

12. Customer responsibilities

The customer must provide accurate contact and vehicle details, share the breakdown location, cooperate with the mechanic and follow reasonable safety instructions. The customer remains responsible for approving additional costs, spare parts, towing and garage work. Fraudulent or misleading use of membership is not permitted.

13. Personal information and records

Customer and vehicle information may be used as necessary to verify membership and payments, maintain service records, contact the customer and coordinate assistance, in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

14. Complaints and support

For membership, payment or service questions, call NRORA at 9160264439. Keep the registered mobile number and payment receipt available when raising a query.

15. Changes and legal rights

NRORA may update these terms prospectively, subject to applicable law. The version shown to the customer at purchase should be retained with the membership record. These terms do not limit consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded.
