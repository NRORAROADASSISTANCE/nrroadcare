Terms & Conditions

1. Service limit: Membership includes up to 3 eligible roadside assistance services during the membership period, subject to service eligibility and coverage.

2. Service only: NRORA provides roadside assistance services. Equipment, spare parts, consumables, repair materials, towing charges or other third-party costs, where applicable, are payable by the customer separately.

3. Coverage: NRORA aims to provide assistance across Telangana through its service network. Actual service availability depends on location, technician availability, road conditions and the applicable service coverage rules.

4. Customer information: Customer and vehicle details must be accurate. Customer-submitted information is used for membership and service coordination.

5. Payment: A payment is considered successful only after confirmation from the payment gateway. No manual payment confirmation is required.
